## Active Question

How does the cortical hierarchy transform information shared with a video foundation model into complementary affectively organized brain representations?

## Critical Brain-JEPA Audit

The Brain-JEPA checkpoint was pretrained on 160 TR (10 temporal patches), whereas
the CCN extraction used 16 TR (one temporal patch). The legacy loader replaced the
checkpoint's 10 fixed sin/cos temporal codes with their mean. This is an arbitrary
adaptation and makes all Brain-JEPA-dependent results provisional.

The affected results are the shared-rank estimate, the Brain-JEPA/V-JEPA2 shared
scores, the shared-channel cortical map, and affective variance conditioned on that
shared channel. The raw-BOLD analyses that condition directly on raw V-JEPA2 and
visual-semantic features are unaffected.

The correction does not require emotion-label finetuning. The primary re-extraction
keeps Brain-JEPA frozen, omits the mismatched fixed checkpoint code, and retains the
native one-patch sin/cos code generated by the model. SwiFT SL20 and NeuroSTORM are
included as matched-window cross-encoder replications. See
`study1/code/brain_encoder_validation/`.
