- **A4-S3 y conditions on the 27 ratings while x conditions on L and O, so the hypothesis does not fix the sign of the slope** (줄 119, 124, 431, 461, 462). x measures head information explained by full captions beyond L and O. y measures head information beyond the 27 ratings. - **A4-P1 H/E/HE partition with banded ridge, 64K vs 27 features and no voxel selection is biased in an SNR-dependent way, so region contrasts are biased** (줄 52, 355, 461, 638). The precedents cited for this partition differ in two ways that matter. Both McMahon and Lescroart used OLS with at most 33 features. Both partitioned only reliability-selected voxels. McMahon also says that regularized regression complicates variance partitioning. Here H has 256 to 768 features and E has 27, fit on about 1,750 training videos. Three consequences follow. (a) Banded ridge selects feature spaces: it heavily shrinks a band whose added value is small. When the H increment is weak, HE comes close to E, H-unique shrinks toward 0, and its variance is relabelled as shared. (b) H alone (high-dimensional) is attenuated more by shrinkage than E alone. (c) How much each part is recovered depends on SNR, and SNR differs between visual and association groups. In a toy simulation (512-d H with effective rank about 40, 27-d E, n = 1,750, shared λ across voxels, banded grid, true joint R² of 0.10, 0.03 and 0.01), estimated H-unique was 42%, 19% and 32% of the true value. E-unique was 87%, 115% and 50%. Shared was 85%, 47% and 19%. In a version with a scaling bug that turned banded ridge into plain ridge, H-unique even came out negative while the true value was positive. 'Association − visual' H-unique differences therefore carry an artefact that is not emotion content. '유효 복셀' is undefined, and the doc does not say whether λ is per voxel or shared. HK used one λ per label set for all voxels.
