### [지지] 4-2 모델 제안, '시험할 때' 단락 마지막 두 문장 (line 217)
- 문서 문장: fMRI에서 시각 모델의 [...] 표상을 복원한 뒤 자극에서 학습한 판독기를 재사용한 선례가 있다. (Two-step structure: fMRI → model features, then a readout trained without brain data, reused unchanged.)
- 근거: Results, 'Neural decoding': "the decoding model included two parts: 1) a multivariate linear regression model that used the fMRI signals to estimate the semantic representation in the 7th (i.e. the 2nd-highest) CNN layer, 2) the built-in transformation from the 7th to the 8th (or output) layer in the CNN, to estimate the categorization probabilities from the decoded semantic representation. The first part of the model was trained with the fMRI data during the training movie; the second part was established by retraining the CNN for image classification into 15 categories." Methods, 'Semantic categorization': "This step utilized the softmax classifier established when retraining the CNN for image classification into 15 labeled categories". Methods, 'Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)': "We used ~20,500 human-labeled images to train the classifier while testing it with a different set of ~3,500 labeled images. The training and testing images were all randomly and evenly sampled from the aforementioned 15 categories in ImageNet". Discussion: "The classifier can be trained without redefining the semantic space, by only retraining the CNN's output layer with labeled images. So, the classifier is separate from the decoding model." (arXiv 1608.03425v2: Results 'Neural decoding' (paragraph beginning 'To identify object categories from fMRI activity'); Methods 'Semantic categorization' and 'Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)'; Discussion 'Direct decoding of semantic representations and categorization')
- 수정 제안: The core structure is supported. One point should be stated explicitly. The '자극' the readout learned from are ~20,500 labeled ImageNet still images in 15 categories. They are not the fMRI movie stimuli. The readout is the CNN's retrained softmax output layer (multinomial logistic regression, KL/cross-entropy loss). In this document's design, the readout is fit to Cowen ratings on the same stimulus set. Suggested wording: '...fMRI로 AlexNet 7층 표상을 선형 회귀(L2)로 추정한 뒤, ImageNet 라벨 이미지로 따로 학습한 15범주 softmax 분류기에 그대로 넣은 선례가 있다(Wen 등, 2018).' The fMRI→layer-7 step was L2-regularized linear regression from visual-cortex voxels ('we used the data during the training movie and applied L2-regularization'), which parallels this document's ridge. Note, however, that the Discussion says the authors generally 'prefer L1 to L2 regularization in training the decoding models'; that statement refers to the layer-1 reconstruction decoders.

### [부분 지지] 4-2 모델 제안, '시험할 때' 단락 (line 217): '시각 모델의 중간 표상'
- 문서 문장: fMRI에서 복원해 판독기에 넣은 것이 시각 모델의 '중간 표상'이다.
- 근거: Methods, CNN: "Note that the 2nd highest layer in the CNN (i.e. the 7th layer) effectively defined a semantic space to support the categorization at the output layer. In other words, the semantic information about the input image was represented by a (4096-dimensional) vector in this semantic space." Fig. 1 caption: "For decoding, cortical responses are combined across locations to estimate the feature outputs from the 1st and 7th layer. The former is deconvolved to reconstruct every movie frame, and the latter is classified into semantic categories." Discussion: "In this study, visual reconstruction was only based on the fMRI-decoded 1st-layer features. Although the feature representations from other layers could also be estimated with comparable accuracies (Supplementary Fig. 8)..." (arXiv 1608.03425v2: Methods 'Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)'; Figure 1 caption; Discussion 'Direct visual reconstruction of a natural movie')
- 수정 제안: What is off: the representation passed to the readout is layer 7 of an 8-layer AlexNet, the penultimate fully connected layer that the authors call the 'semantic space'. It feeds directly into the output layer. In the paper's terms it is not a mid-level layer. It counts as 'intermediate' only in the loose sense of a hidden layer. The other decoded layer (layer 1) went to De-CNN pixel reconstruction, not to a readout. Wen et al. therefore give no precedent for a stimulus-trained readout on a mid-level layer. That matters because line 213 of the document anticipates choosing '중간층 특징'. Replace '중간 표상' with '끝에서 두 번째 층(7층, 4096차원 의미 공간) 표상'.
